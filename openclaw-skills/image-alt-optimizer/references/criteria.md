# Common SEO Criteria

## Severity scale

- Critical: Likely prevents indexing, ranking, conversion, or causes serious brand/reputation risk.
- High: Likely materially suppresses performance and has a clear fix.
- Medium: Meaningful improvement opportunity, but not the primary blocker.
- Low: Nice-to-have or hygiene improvement.
- No issue found: Checked and acceptable.
- Unknown: Could not verify with available access/data.

## Evidence requirements

Every issue should include:
- URL or entity checked
- Tool/source used
- Observed value or finding
- Why it matters
- Recommended fix
- Confidence level
