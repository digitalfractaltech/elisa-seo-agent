# Gotchas

- Do not confuse navigation/sidebar/footer links with contextual links in main content.
- Do not add exact-match anchors repeatedly.
- Do not treat category, tag, author, archive, search, login, cart, checkout, privacy, terms, maps, or contact pages as normal content pages.
- Do not assume a page is indexable until HTTP status, canonical, robots meta, X-Robots-Tag, robots.txt, and sitemap inclusion are checked where relevant.
- Do not force entities into copy if readability suffers.
- Do not use unavailable GBP data. Mark it unknown.
- Do not mention internal tool names in client-facing PDF/report outputs unless the report is internal.
- Do not rebuild or reorder manifests during resumable batch workflows unless the approver explicitly asks.
