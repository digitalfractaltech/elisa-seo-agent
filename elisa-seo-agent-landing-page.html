SEO has become too complex to manage manually.

Modern SEO is no longer just titles, meta descriptions, and blog posts. Teams now need to understand rankings, site architecture, topical coverage, internal links, page quality, AI visibility, competitor entities, and technical issues — continuously.

  • Too many moving parts: keywords, pages, internal links, rankings, content gaps, SERP features, and technical signals.
  • Too much guessing: generic AI prompts often produce generic advice because they do not use live SERP evidence.
  • Too little tracking: teams often make changes without a baseline or clear measurement window.
  • Too much manual work: sitemap audits, page checks, and internal linking take hours when done by hand.

ELISA turns SEO into an agent-led operating system.

The system coordinates specialized AI skills that gather evidence, decide the next best task, create audit trails, and prepare changes for human approval.

1

Onboard the project

ELISA collects the website, sitemap, money keywords, geography, competitors, approval rules, CMS access, and data-source status. Every project gets its own Obsidian vault.

2

Establish the baseline

DataForSEO captures explicit Google organic rankings. Google Search Console supplies impressions, clicks, CTR, and average position. ELISA stores snapshots over time.

3

Map the site

The system audits the sitemap, classifies top-level money pages, secondary pages, location-oriented pages, blogs, and support content into a clear SEO hierarchy.

4

Find keyword-based link opportunities

ELISA catalogues keyword mentions already present in pages and posts, maps them to target money pages, and creates contextual internal linking opportunities.

5

Optionally enhance with live SERP data

When connected, On-Page.ai can add competitor-driven entity gaps, related words, page structure benchmarks, topical alignment, speed signals, and internal-link recommendations. The core workflow can still run without it.

6

Draft, verify, and report

Approved changes can be prepared as CMS drafts. ELISA tracks what was done, why it was done, the expected goal, and whether performance improves or degrades.

The workflow: from discovery to measurable improvement.

ELISA is designed to reduce scattered SEO activity and replace it with a repeatable, evidence-based operating loop.

STEP 01

Onboard

Create the project vault, collect business goals, data access, money keywords, and approval rules.

STEP 02

Baseline

Capture initial rankings, GSC traffic, target URLs, and keyword-page mapping.

STEP 03

Audit

Map the sitemap, inspect page hierarchy, scan priority pages, and identify gaps.

STEP 04

Implement

Prepare approved internal links, on-page fixes, alt text, headings, and content refreshes as drafts.

STEP 05

Measure

Run weekly checks, compare against baseline, and summarize improvements, degradations, and next actions.

Designed for security, memory, and accountability.

ELISA is built for real business operations, where access control, audit trails, and project separation matter.

Project-separated memory

Each client or website has its own Obsidian vault. ELISA does not mix project context across clients.

Local credential control

API credentials remain in local, gitignored configuration and are never written to the project vault, reports, logs, or exports. Bitwarden can be added optionally when a team wants managed secret storage.

Human approval gates

Publishing, money-page edits, redirects, canonicals, and public content changes require approval unless explicitly pre-approved.
